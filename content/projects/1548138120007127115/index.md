I'm making a presence sensor, and I had GPT 6 Astra design a little box for it. I described what I wanted in a few messages and this was the first thing it generated, in SCAD. I didn't write a line of code or touch any CAD.

I have never had anything I made work so well first time. It's press-fit, and everything fit together perfectly: the ESP, the sensor, the lid, all of it, first time.

I did a few iterations later to change stuff, and after those iterations it fits perfectly.
