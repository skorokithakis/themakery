This is my head, scanned, turned into an egg, scaled up and printed. It also acts as a bathroom companion.

I did the scanning with the Heges app on the iPhone, which uses the FaceID camera. Turning the scan into an egg was done in Blender, as a manual process, though something like Astra might be able to help with that. The shape of my head lends itself to the egg form, so a different head might need a different approach.

I try to keep my scans away from the internet, just in case.
