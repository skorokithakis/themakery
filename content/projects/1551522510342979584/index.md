This is my head, scanned, turned into an egg, scaled up and printed.

I did the scanning with the Heges app on the iPhone, which uses the FaceID camera. Android probably has something similar. Turning the scan into an egg was done in Blender, and it was a manual process, though maybe Astra can help with that. The shape of my head lends itself to the egg form, so someone else's scan might want a different shape.

I do try to keep my scans away from the internet, just in case.
