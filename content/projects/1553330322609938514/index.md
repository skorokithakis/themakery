Stavros had used plenty of travel planners, and every one of them was missing something: good AI integration, perhaps, or a good visualisation of the trip, or how long it takes to travel between places. So he set about making his own, a travel planning website called Wherewhen.

He shared a view of its current state, admitting that "the UI is a bit shit still" because he hasn't yet done a pass on it. He plans to tidy it up once the functionality is right.

The functionality is already coming along. A calendar view lets times be adjusted by dragging, and it shows driving and walking times between places. Ideas can be added and then placed on a map later. The site also supports collaborative editing, so friends can be invited to see and edit a trip together, and it works completely offline, thanks to Yjs. As Stavros put it, "CRDTs are pretty great."
